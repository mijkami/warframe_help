    Steel Path Warframes

    Focus on a very small pool of warframes at the beginning, preferably with good built-in survival tools without too much investment.

    Force yourself to read the full descriptions of the frame's kit (Arsenal > Hover over Frame > Abilities: read passive, abilities and all tips).

    Consider adding a Helminth for damage or survival.

    RECOMMENDED FRAMES

    The selection is already reduced, to push further the top prio / super-meta / easy are indicated in bold:

    Prio: autonomous damage & status resistance

    Dante, Revenant, Kullervo, Follie

    Nezha, Rhino, Valkyr, Trinity, Gauss, Atlas, Uriel

    Very good: damage resistance + need to add status resistance

    • add status resistance externally:
      • Nova, Protea, Wisp, Baruuk, Hildryn, Ash, Citrine, Nyx, Yareli, Xaku, Octavia
    • special case: Lavos (passive + Synth Fiber to remove cooldown)

    Bonus choices

    • Rush Spy/Capture: Wukong, Titania, Voruna (bonus velocity)
    • Weapon platform: Saryn, Mirage, Volt, Oraxia
    • others: in principle all frames are viable, those listed can just make Steel Path access easier and are less demanding than others in terms of survival/DPS/modding.

    FRAME MODS: SURVIVE

    • health, armor & Adaptation mod:
      • without arcane: for early Steel Path this can help pass a threshold. Damage reduction skills can also help. After that it peters out (lvl 1500+ in endless missions) but that's not a problem here.
      • with Arcane Persistence: health/armor tanking viable at lvl cap. Prerequisites:
        • minimum 700 armor for activation. Count 1400 armor to handle heat or corrosive procs (1892 for proc+corro at same time but very rare). Consider blue archon shards.
        • take into account magnetic status and nullifiers that can disable the arcane (!):
          • status: either use cleanses (Saryn's Molt, Rolling Guard mod), or status immunity (Inaros' Scarab Armor, Trinity's Link, Titania's Helminth Spellbind / Trinity's Well of Life, etc...)
          • nullifiers: have enough AoE or melee weapons with lots of range and/or impact status. Consider a fast-firing weapon or one that can cancel shields (Miter with Neutralizing Justice)
    • Shieldgating:
      • Catalyzing Shields + Brief Respite / Augur mods: active/dynamic gameplay and energy-demanding, so need good management (Flow + Equilibrium and/or little Efficiency and/or Helminth Nourish from Grendel, for example)
      • Primed Redirection + Guardian (companion mod): expensive technique, complements with Vigilante Vigor + Fast Deflection or even Arcane Aegis if you want to push it. For the companion with Guardian, prefer a Diriga (Arc Coil + Guardian + Manifold Bond + Momentous Bond + (Primed) Regen, NO Assault Mode) and a weapon full of status (Prime Laser Rifle + Viral/Fire/Mag/Rad)
      • Omamori (Helminth) + Catalyzing Shields + Vigilante Vigor + Fast Deflection +/- Arcane Aegis: quasi-invulnerability passive as long as Omamori is maintained
    • Invisibility: very strong but sensitive to stray bullets (bad positioning) and damage over time (Rolling Guard compensates)
      • Frame ability: Ash, Ivara, Loki, Octavia, Oraxia, Voruna, Wisp, Wukong, Cyte-09
      • Helminth:
        • Evade from Cyte, little compatible with group play
        • Quiver from Ivara, reserved for static or haphazard gameplay (possible to plant the arrow in your sentinel)
      • Companions: for "caster" frames that don't need to use weapons (Protea, Xaku, Dante...)
        • Shade Prime: more solid (less aggro), access to Guardian mod
        • Huras Kubrow: free companion that can die more easily, gives access to retriever mods + Kubrow nuke

    ENERGY

    In most cases, running out of energy is synonymous with death: either you no longer kill dangerous enemies, or you no longer cast skills to feed shieldgating.

    Two main strategies that can be combined:

    • reduce the cost of skills:
    • efficiency: direct reduction
      • Arcane Sculptor
        • frame (very) compatible: Cyte, Follie, Gara, Gyre, Jade, Protea, Vauban, Volt
        • helminth (very) compatible: Brightbonnet (Nokko), Molt (Saryn), Astral Shell (Sirius & Orion)
    • duration: for buffs, fewer casts / minute => less energy spent
    • increase the stock/energy pool and ways to refill it
    • energy pool: Flow, Primed Flow, blue archon shards (2-3 max)
    • energy supply:
      • common / meta:
        • drop + conversion health orbs: companion AoE + Synth Deconstruct + health > energy conversion (Equilibrium, purple shards)
        • helminth skill:
          • Nourish from Grendel, combo possible with Energy Nexus and/or Archon Stretch (proc with Arc Coil from Diriga or elec on companion claws)
          • Brightbonnet from Nokko. The rebound of the projectile from the skill or frame on the mushrooms created allows speeding up energy regeneration. Think about "spamming" against a wall to make it rebound on mushrooms very quickly. Excellent combo with Arcane Sculptor.
        • Arcane Energize most expensive/difficult to farm today but very effective (prioritize reroll Vosfor, event drops or plats... Eidolon runs as last resort)
        • kill an Eximus: guaranteed energy orb drop
        • Large Energy Restore ("pizza"): bind to a shortcut in the consumable wheel + keyboard/gamepad shortcut. Can be spammed in normal, cooldown in SP. x100 craft blueprint available in Dojo.
      • uncommon:
        • archon shard yellow: +50% efficiency on energy orbs (+75% for tau-forged)
        • Parazon: make a finisher with Blood for Energy
        • Non-Helminth Skills
        • Duplex Bond + companion that hits hard in melee
        • Focus school Zenurik Energy Pulse (incompatible with certain game modes requiring Madurai)
        • Arcane Primary Exhilarate on main weapon primer (ex: Bubonico with forced impact + AoE)
        • Arcane Emergence Dissipate for operator
        • Energy Generator from Dethcube companion
        • Xata Invocation from the Grimoire (secondary weapon)
        • Rage / Hunter Adrenaline: losing health often means getting one-shot at a certain point in Steel Path, avoid except in special cases
        • Entropy effect from Cephalon Suda faction: passively by gaining XP while carrying weapons/mods with the effect, generates 25% max energy for 30s and gives 25% energy. Notably Entropy Burst from Supra Vandal which can make a energy regen rifle and anti-nullifier shield weapon thanks to high firerate.

    HELMINTH

    Unlock Helminth on Deimos to apply abilities to other frames and free up frame slots without uselessly "throwing away" them.

    Priority Helminth Abilities

    Very strong, common & meta:

    • damage (stat buffs):
      • Roar from Rhino to boost anti-faction, synergizes with damage over time (fire, blast, toxin, elec)
      • Xata from Xaku to add Void damage (many interactions + Void bonus damage on overguard + magnetic bubble effect)
      • Nourish from Grendel to boost energy gain, Viral bonus, light CC
      • Wrathful Advance from Kullervo to buff melee crit %, reverse the cast via preferences and aim at the ground
    • tanking:
      • Pillage from Hildryn for shield gain + armor/shield strip + cleanse status
      • Omamori from Koumei for pseudo-dodge (very strong in addition to dodge/shield-gating)
    • armor strip: less priority today, makes enemies more fragile:
      • Pillage from Hildryn, shield and armor strip, requires lots of power (rather with Corrosive Projection aura) + blocked by walls, long cast
      • Terrify from Nekros, armor only, no LoS, requires little power, fast cast, costs quite a lot of energy
      • Tharros Strike from Styanax, armor and shield strip, LoS, fast cast, heals health, short range
      • Ophanim Eyes from Jade, armor only, continuous debuff in vision cone, little energy consumed + enemy slow

    Niche Helminth Abilities

    Sometimes useful, much less visible/prevalent:

    • damage (stat buffs):
      • Eclipse: increases base damage (like a Serration mod). Heavily nerfed in the past, can be useful. Multiplicative damage buff applied after the rest. Double bonus for melee projectiles (Exodia Contagion, Glaives)
      • Thermal Sunder from Gauss + augment mod Thermal Transfer to add Blast
      • Shock from Volt + Shock Trooper: electric damage boost
      • Warcry from Valkyr: melee attack speed buff (mostly) and armor (a bit)
      • Breach Surge from Wisp: multiplies damage received from one target to inflict on another, light CC (radiation + blind, does not affect Eximus)
      • Airburst from Zephyr + Airbust Rounds: secondary weapon damage buff, requires hitting many targets (range) often an anti-synergy
    • damage (debuff):
      • Webbed Embrace from Oraxia: zone CC (3 zones max) + vulnerability debuff (= increased damage received)
      • Sentient Wrath from Caliban: cone CC + vulnerability debuff
      • Rest & Rage from Equinox (Day version, requires a clear emission color on the frame): enemy speed boost + vulnerability debuff
    • tanking:
    • buff / frame effect:
      • Molt from Saryn: light aggro (instant part), speed boost, status cleanse & instant cast (useful for shield-gating)
      • Null Star from Nova: 75% DR + enemy CC
      • Condemn from Harrow: shield regen + enemy CC + easy headshots
      • Quiver from Ivara, allows creating static invisibility zones. Very particular gameplay. Possibility to link the zone to a sentinel, hard to achieve consistently in dangerous situations
      • Eclipse: 75% damage reduction. Can be shared with allies via Total Eclipse
    • enemy control:
      • Resonator from Octavia, completely disables enemy AI. Requires lots of range
      • Astral Shell from Sirius & Orion: automatically generates when hit invulnerable decoys that draw fire to them within 30m. Couple with a bit of shield regen. Compatible with Arcane Sculptor
      • Silence from Banshee: disables enemy abilities (including Eximus auras) + short stun
      • Shooting Gallery from Mesa + Muzzle Flash: very good CC but no longer affects Eximus, small damage buff too
      • Gloom from Sevagoth, important slow + life steal, big energy drain
      • Webbed Embrace from Oraxia: zone CC (3 zones max) + vulnerability debuff (= increased damage received)
    • energy: (other than Nourish...)
      • Dispensary from Protea: generation of health/energy orbs + ammo at a fixed point
      • Fractured Blast from Citrine for health/energy orb generation
      • Lycath's Hunt from Voruna for health orb generation on melee attacks / energy on headshots (use Equilibrium + melee)

    STATUS RESISTANCE

    Status from enemies has 3 main dangers:

    • damage over time: especially toxin, fire, slash
    • controls: the most annoying are Knockdown and Stagger blocking you in place
    • energy drain via Magnetic status, less common but potentially deadly

    You can protect yourself in different ways via immunities or cleanses/removals. Requires specific frame kits or external sources (Helminth, mods, companions, arcanes).

    Control Immunity:

    • Not mandatory, strongly recommended. In Steel Path there are + more Eximus and a greater density of mobs: + dangerous controls. Being immune prevents dying while a mob knocks you down without being able to react
    • Have a frame immunized via skills or Overguard (see recommended frames)
    • Use Primed Sure Footed or Handspring for controls
    • avoid Sure Footed (non-prime)
    • if PSF impossible, prefer Handspring, only takes one mod
    • Sure Footed (non-prime) + Fortitude possible for 100% immunity but takes one slot and "breaks" your builds, rarely feasible (unless survival already based on passive shield regen)
    • Negate from Wyrm in combo with Manifold Bond to reduce cooldown. Requires being very comfortable with DPS and being able to keep your companion alive / bring it back quickly (Momentous Bond + Primed Regen and others)

    MOBILITY

    Maneuvers are the basis of Warframe movement: bullet-jumps, rolls, slides... reference video

    Fundamental part of the game for:

    • the fun of the game
    • movement speed
    • survivability
    • the roll provides 75% damage reduction, or even invulnerability via Rolling Guard
    • fast movement greatly reduces the probability of being hit / enemy accuracy
    • Survival Instinct from Sly Vulpaphyla even resets enemy targeting AI every 5 seconds via a bullet jump

    Common sources:

    • Velocity: major impact, increases bullet-jump and roll distances
      • Praedos: 30% (+speed boost)
      • Archon shard yellow: +15 / +22.5%
      • velocity mods: relatively niche, can replace PSF in exilus on some builds if you have invulnerability or are comfortable
      • exilus Proton Pulse for experienced Cascade players
      • arcanes (more rare) Agility & Consequence
    • Sprint Speed
      • Praedos +20% (OK + velocity speed boost)
      • Innodem / Okina incarnon: +30% (max for melee)
      • exilus melee: Dispatch Overdrive: +60% after heavy attack (mostly slam builds)
      • Amalgam Serration: for main weapon
      • Helminth: Molt: multiple uses with status cleanse + speed + instant energy spending for shieldgating
    • Dodge Speed: only one source, Amalgam Barrel Diffusion on secondary weapon
    • Slide: minor impact
