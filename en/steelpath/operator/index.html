    Operator

    Focus

    • Unairu allows you to be immune to certain controls (Poise, first talent) and self-rez (Last Gasp)
    • Vazarin can make you invincible (Protective Sling)
    • Zenurik for energy regen if needed (find other sources as soon as possible)
    • Madurai useful for operator damage later in the game (boost Tenno damage + power/speed cast for frame)

    Survival

    The operator can contribute to warframe survival:

    • self-resurrection:
      • via the talent Last Gasp of the Unairu Focus school
      • allows bringing the warframe back to life by killing 3 enemies as operator within 15 seconds
      • available for all operator branches: unlock the passives linked to the path concerned to access them permanently (max rank). Other Way-Bounds passives allow increasing operator damage & survival making resurrection easier.
      • Madurai active skills facilitate operator kills for rez
      • the glaive (Phahd, secondary attack) from the amplifier facilitates rez
    • preventive protection:
      • via the Vazarin talent Protective Sling
      • allows making allied targets (including your warframe) invulnerable for 5s after "dashing" through
      • use Magus Cloud to facilitate the operation
      • very particular / repetitive gameplay & requires Tektolyst unlocked for lvl cap (enemies lvl 9999)
    • enemy control:
      • via the operator arcane Magus Lockdown
      • an operator dash allows creating a mine (max 2) that blocks up to 10 enemies each for 4 seconds within 15 meters

    Amplifiers

    We describe amplifiers by the number of the Prism, Scaffold and Brace parts, example 111 = Raplak Pencha Clapkra. Meta amplifiers:

    Code Part Names Utility Usage
    123 Raplak Shraksun Lohrin get started (just Certus rep) beginner
    177 Raplak Propa Certus single target / launch an AoE for Vomvalysts pref Eidolons
    147 Raplak Phahd Certus single target / launch a glaive for self rez pref Cascade
    547 Cantic Phahd Certus single target (multishot) / launch a glaive pref Cascade
    747 Klamora Phahd Certus multi-target / launch a glaive SteelPath comfort + OK Cascade

    Arcanes

    This will mainly be the operator that sometimes needs to be adapted to the content.

    • operator:
      • cascade: Magus Cloud + other (ex: Magus Lockdown)
      • disruption: Magus Lockdown + other
      • others:
        • Magus Melt: +damage for Zariman Angels + operator rez
        • Magus Anomaly: attracts enemies, useful for certain bounties/missions (Deimos)
        • Emergence Dissipate: energy regen
    • amp:
      • 1st slot mandatory: Eternal Eradicate
      • 2nd slot to choose from:
        • Eternal Onslaught
        • Virtuous Strike
        • Virtuous Shadow
        • Eternal Logistics

    Tektolyst / Tauron Artifacts

    Tektolyst artifacts are "ultimate weapons" for operators:

    • give access to new mods and arcanes applying to operator weapons:
      • big damage boost, makes "viable" all operator schools at lvl cap (enemies lvl 9999). Without Tektolyst, Madurai is "mandatory" for Cascade cap.
      • arcanes impacting warframe weapons (fire boost on secondary weapons)
      • buffs and ultimate attacks interesting but too random / little used as is
    • accessible after completing the quest The Old Peace, in drops from the gameplay mode Rebellion Perita and Marie's shop

    Tektolyst Modding Generalist

    • Mods:
      • Ubri-Kaneph: base damage
      • Omni-Evi: crit chance / crit damage
      • Hok-Kaal: operator control protection
      • Hayan-Dabor: multishot
      • Vikla-Safor: Fire Rate + Ammo Efficiency
    • Arcane: Zid-An Uskos: Operator kills give a permanent buff up to +250% fire damage on secondary weapon (of warframe)
