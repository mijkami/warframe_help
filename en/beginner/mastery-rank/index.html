    Mastery Rank

    General

    • there are currently 35 account mastery ranks in the game.
    • most weapons/frames require a minimum Mastery Rank (MR). At level 16 you have access to 99% of content.
    • maxing to 30/30 an item gives you the maximum mastery for your account.
    • think about maxing "trash" items whose blueprints only cost a few credits and that you will want to throw away later to free up a slot
    • build as many weapons and warframes as possible to level up mastery, you can throw away the easiest to obtain
    • do NOT throw away your non-prime frames directly, farm helminth on Deimos first, you will need to feed it your non-prime frames
    • you can build an item in advance and pick it up from the foundry much later
    • consult Farm for the resources and XP needed for your new frames & weapons

    Mainly unlocks:

    • Quests (rank 3, 4, 5, 6)
    • Trading (rank 2)
    • Syndicates (rank 3)
    • Sorties (rank 5)
    • access Kuva Liches and Corpus Liches as well as their weapons (rank 5)
    • purchase Helminth Segment (rank 8)

    Each rank also unlocks:

    • +1 trade max
    • +50 void traces max threshold
    • +500 daily syndicate reputation max
    • +5000 daily focus max

    Blueprints / Blueprints

    The crux of the matter. Many sources of acquisition, always have items in construction in your foundry to regularly increase your Mastery.

    /!\ Some blueprints require other weapons. Do NOT throw away a non-prime weapon until you've checked the list above!

    • market: the market in your orbiter gives access to lots of weapons, even if the price is shown in plats, click on it and buy the plan/blueprint in credits. Some basic weapons can be bought already built for credits
    • dojo: joining a clan gives access to different labs and a lot of weapons and some warframes. Think about doing Invasions to obtain rare resources for some weapons.
    • boss: all bosses drop frame plans or even weapon plans, think about doing them multiple times to get all necessary plans
    • reputation: syndicates have reputations that will give you access to weapons and parts
    • open-worlds: special syndicates asking to do bounties / bounty missions in open worlds, bounties drops and reputations offer lots of weapon and frame plans
