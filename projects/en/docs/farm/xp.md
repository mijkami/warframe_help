# Experience

## **XP Gains**

XP affects [Mastery Rank](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mastery_Rank).

[XP gains](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Affinity) are shared as follows:

- external sources (ex: ally kills): 25% to frame and 75% to weapons. Equipping one weapon allows leveling it faster.
- frame kills: 100% to frame
- weapon kills: 50% to frame 50% to the weapon used

Top XP sources:

- Sentinel companions (one companion + 1 Sentinel weapon) = 6000 + 3000 XP
- warframes = 6000 XP
- companions = 6000 XP
- weapons = 3000 XP
- etc

-------------------------

## **Boosters**

The XP booster from the shop is not necessary but appreciated.
Later you can also get a [Smeeta Kavat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Smeeta_Kavat) which gives a 300% XP buff via its mod [Charm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Charm)

See [companions](../beginner/pets.md) for more info.

-------------------------

## **Good Spots**

You will rarely use all your weapons, think about equipping unused slots with weapons you want to level up passively while playing.

Limit equipped weapons to focus XP gain on 1 or 2 weapons.

- **beginner**:
    - **public**
        - Helene, Saturn (Defense)
        - Sanctuary / Elite Sanctuary (unlocked after quest [The New Strange](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_Strange)):
            - ally XP for leveling weapons (let them kill a bit)
            - your own kills for leveling frames: put Gauss's helminth on the frame's ability 1 + caster mods (Flow, max range, max power)
        - Archwing: Salacia, Neptune (Mobile Defense)
- **confirmed**:
    - **public**:
        - [Elara](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aw33zyUmADU) on Neptune with a crewmate equipped, select Asian servers
    - **solo**:
        - Eidolon Plains [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z6pQMaYj-EE&t=964s&pp=ygURZWxhcmZyYW1lJ2V4cCBwYXJ0aXM%3D), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rcd0RE3IG6A)
