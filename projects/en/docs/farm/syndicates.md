Different reputations in the game.


## **Base Syndicates**

- **who?**: Hexis, Suda, Red Veil, Steel Meridian, New Loka, Perrin Sequence
- **where?**: your orbiter, left of your navigation
- **how?**:
    - you must "pledge" / align with one syndicate as soon as possible to passively increase your standing
    - each syndicate has one ally and 2 enemies: staying on one syndicate allows leveling another one passively
        - it is possible to have 4 maxed syndicates but it's longer term (level one syndicate + its ally, then level another that is not enemy with your 2 syndicates, its ally will also level)
    - leveling up ranks requires sacrifices (reactor, catalyst, aya...) but go for it! It will bring you much more plat in the end than you spend, and quickly!

## **Open-World Syndicates**

- **who?**: **Cetus**, **Fortuna**, **Entrati**
- **where?**: Earth, Venus, Necralisk (Deimos)
- **how?**:
    - do bounties / missions, any of them
    - others:
        - animal conservation (Fortuna / Cetus, fastest long-term)
        - see [Steel Path Farm section](https://gist.github.com/mijkami/c5789f0d6f59893d58d149f30939b26a#farm) for more info

## **Mini-Hub Syndicates**

- **who?**: Holdfast, Cavia, The Hex
- **where?**: **Chrysalith** (**Zariman**), **Sanctum** Anatomica (**Deimos**), **Höllvania Central Mall** (**1999**) 
- **how?**:
    - buy a [**Parallax**](https://warframe.market/items/parallax_set) (cheap, very profitable) to be able to "scan" parts of the map and easily earn Ayatan statues and reputation medallions
    - [Fortuna](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_xkKO-4VqCI) / Cetus: Helminth Equinox (sleep) or Equinox (pref Titania) in good spots (see Fortuna link, look for Kavats at night for Cetus)
    - Zariman / Sanctum Anatomica (Deimos):
        - monitor bounties/missions
        - favor max rank (4 or 5) fast bounties (extermination + simple secondary condition)
        - pre-farming possible with Zariman (only feather gain). Use Parallax (see above)
