Mission objectives: to follow as a priority

- indicated by a yellow (or white) indicator on your screen
- detailed at the top left of your screen (ex: "join objective" / "kill X enemies")

We will talk here about the most [common missions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mission) at the beginning. Other special missions will be available later.

#### Short Missions

- Capture
- Rescue
- Extermination
- Spy
- Mobile Defense
- Sabotage
- Hijack

#### Long Missions
"Endless" infinite missions. In [Fissure](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Fissure) to open relics, each rotation allows you to accumulate boosters (credits, resources, drops) at each rotation up to +100%.

- Defense
- Interception
- Excavation
- Disruption
- etc...
