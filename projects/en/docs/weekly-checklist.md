## **Confirmed Players**

Requires having completed all main quests up to Hex and maxed Cavia & Hex rep.

!!! note "5x Netracells"
    Different ways to spend your 5 weekly charges:

    - 2 x **Archimedean Elite** Temporal + Deep (2x2 charges): **priority**. Source of **Archon Shards**.
        - Do runs in the evening at the start or end of the week to have more people with you
        - 1 charge remaining = 1 netracell
    - OR 5x [netracells](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Netracells) if you struggle in Archimedean, to avoid but lesser evil

- __Weekly Sortie__: Archon Hunt => **Archon Shards, sometimes Tau** (Navigation, last tab)
- __Zariman__: visit the Archimedean Yonta and buy **Kuva** for 5 feathers (farm feathers beforehand)
- __Earth__:
    - Iron Wake: visit Paladino to buy **Kuva** + credits + 2 veiled rivens
    - Drifter camp: Kahl mission
- __Deimos__:
    - visit Bird-3 in the Sanctum Anatomica to buy an **Archon Shard** (farm rep beforehand, NB: Archimedean/netracells give rep)
    - **Archimedean Deep Elite** (2 charges): **priority**. Source of **basic Archon Shards + tau**.
        - Possible to remove a handicap: focus on a hyper-op frame as a joker (Dante, Octavia) and take everything else. 
    - 1 x [netracell](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Netracells). Only for charges not spent on Archimedean

- [__Descendia__](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Descendia):
    - interest (especially Steel Path version) depends on weekly rewards, notably:
        - occasional Archon Shard
        - [Mod Drop Chance Booster](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mod_Drop_Chance_Booster) which helps farming certain mods (ex Orb Vallis) and arcanes (Cascade drops)
        - Reactor/Catalyst Blueprint: equivalent to 20 plat in store, see if it's worth the 25-35mn for a full rotation or farm something else
    - normal version interest: potential [farm](farm/plats.en.md#good-deals) arcanes / vosphor


- __1999__:
    - Archimedean Temporal Elite (2 charges): **priority**. Source of **basic Archon Shards + tau**. Take all handicaps for maximum loot. Do runs in the evening at the start or end of the week to have more people with you.
    - Calendar
        - favor nodes giving **Archon Shards**, reactors, catalysts
        - fill completely if needed to facilitate temporal Archimedean

- __Duviri__:
    - [Steelpath Circuit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Circuit#The_Steel_Path_Circuit): **priority**, choose **Incarnon weapon adapters** between what is strong/meta and what interests you. See [Incarnons](steelpath/weapons.md#incarnons) section or weekly recaps from [GazTTV](https://www.youtube.com/@GazTTV) (does a weekly review of Incarnons with advice for selection)
