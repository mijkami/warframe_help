## Damage: Essential Stats

Identify the best synergies for your weapons and [stack multipliers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FHOXcDAOUiY).

!!! note "base damage formula"

    _base damage_ X _elemental damage_ X _crit chance_ X _crit damage_

Remember to hover your cursor over the different stats on the left to understand their details, especially status stats corresponding to infused status.

- **base damage**: increases all weapon damage (Serrated, Barrel, Pressure Points), stat that will be multiplied by many other mods. Mandatory.
- ??? note "elemental damage"
    - multiplies base damage
    - putting two status together merges them to give a combined status
    - the game reads mod slots from left to right and top to bottom like a book: arrange mods in this order to get desired combinations
        - example 1: "toxin + electricity + fire" will give "**corrosive (tox + elec) + fire**"
        - example 2: "fire + toxin + electricity" will give "**gas (fire + tox) + electricity**"
    - enemies have [different positive/negative resistances](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage#Overview_Table) to damage, ex:
        - [corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) (toxin + electricity) deals +50% damage to Grineer
        - [toxin](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Toxin_Damage) ignores shields and attacks health directly. Very effective against Corpus which have little health and lots of shields.
    - gives access to elements sometimes having important effects for damage:
        - [viral](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Viral_Damage) (toxin + cold): increases damage to health by 100 to 325% (10 stacks max per target for max effect)
        - [magnetic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage) (cold + electricity): increases damage to shields and overguard (Eximus, Thrax) by 100 to 325%, 10 stacks
        - [corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) (toxin + electricity): reduces enemy armor by 26 to 80% at 10 stacks. Possibility to increase stacks later via [archon shards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard)
        - [heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage): deals damage over time per stack + reduces armor by 15 to 50% (max effect after 5s of burn). No max stacks, increases as long as it's applied
- ??? note "crit chance"
    - increases chances of [critical hit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Hit) multiplying base and elemental damage
    - can exceed 100% for big crits (orange) or even super crits (red+). We talk about "crit tiers".
    - mods increase the weapon's base crit stats. A weapon with low crit (<20% or so) benefits little from crit mods
- **crit damage**: multiplier applied to crits to make them even more massive

!!! note "status choice"

    - if in doubt for status to use, start with **Viral + Heat** or **Corro + Heat** (big generalization but it works)
    - for more details consult this [video explaining status and their strengths](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ghYu61eRdPE)

Sometimes weapons have high status chance and/or low crits, you can then focus on another stat:

- ??? note "status chance"
    - increases status chance. Can exceed 100% (gives additional procs)
    - different types of [status effects](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect):
      - debuff:
        - damage received debuff: viral, magnetic
        - stat debuff (armor, other): corrosive, heat, cold, puncture
        - CC debuff (crowd control): cold, radiation, heat, electricity, impact, blast
      - damage over time (DoT): heat, electricity, toxin, gas, blast, slash

## **Primary & Secondary Weapons**

Some stats are exclusive to primary/secondary weapons:

- [multishot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Multishot): increases the number of bullets fired per shot. Multiplies all other damage stats
    - Primaries: [Split Chamber](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Split_Chamber)
    - Secondaries: [Barrel Diffusion](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Barrel_Diffusion) + [Lethal Torrent](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lethal_Torrent)
- fire rate: rate of fire, allows increasing DPS at the cost of worse ammo economy

## **CO: Condition Overload, GunCO**

!!! note "Big damage boost potential"

    We talk about CO for all [mechanics similar to Condition Overload mod](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload_(Mechanic)): mods giving a damage bonus based on the amount of different status present on a target.

Affected mods: [Condition Overload](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload), [Galvanized Aptitude](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Galvanized_Aptitude), [Galvanized Shot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Galvanized_Shot), [Galvanized Savvy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Galvanized_Savvy) + some weapon passives (Incarnon mainly).

- by default the damage bonus from these mods is **additive** with your base damage
- in some cases the bonus from these mods is **multiplicative** with your base damage

Outside of the classic use with a [primer](../steelpath/weapons.md/#priming) which works well with the default functionality, very high damage can be achieved when the weapon works multiplicatively with this mod.

If you don't have the faith to test each weapon to see which category it falls into, consult the [community GunCO sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ryemX4Y2vWy9LjuJ355bWVNuBhzLaHTTFqPeTNto9RA).

## **Melee Weapons**
The same base logic applies: "base damage X elemental damage X crit chance X crit damage".

!!! note "Melee Stats Synergy"
    
    base damage X elemental damage X attack speed/range X crit chance X crit damage

- melee scaling mods: 
      - priority: Blood Rush, Weeping Wounds, Condition Overload
      - others: to a lesser extent [Gladiator Set](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gladiator_Set) (we like [Gladiator Might](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gladiator_Might) but it's often outclassed by [Galvanized Steel](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Galvanized_Steel))
- check if the weapon already has high base **status %** or **crit %** and mod accordingly (boosting a low value is not interesting)
- stance:
    - adds capacity like frame auras
    - allows varying attacks and combos as well as damage dealt by combos (some stances are more enjoyable to play and/or hit harder, for example [Sovereign Outcast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sovereign_Outcast) which dominates tonfas)
- [attack speed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Attack_Speed): [Fury](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fury), [Quickening](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quickening) & [Berserker Fury](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Berserker_Fury) are affordable
- range: ([Primed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primed_Reach)) [Reach](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reach), allows hitting enemies from further / more enemies at once




### **Glaive Weapons**
[Glaives](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Glaive) are very special melee weapons.
You throw them with a heavy attack and can make them explode at distance with a heavy/secondary attack.

From Mastery Rank 4 you can obtain a Xoris which is an excellent glaive.

Mods used on them are quite particular:

- gain combo via [Corrupt Charge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corrupt_Charge) to increase explosion damage
- specific mods for explosion:
    - [Volatile Quick Return](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Volatile_Quick_Return) for a single explosion with great range (very common)
    - [Volatile Rebound](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Volatile_Rebound) for an explosion on each bounce (especially for Falcor glaive)
    - mods that do NOT affect explosion: range, attack speed

## **Weapon Mods: Survival**
Uncommon, survival sources in weapons can complement a setup/loadout too fragile otherwise (ex: frame completely oriented towards damage buff)

- [**Arcane Secondary Fortifier**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Secondary_Fortifier):
    - allows secondary weapons to steal up to 15,000 overguard from enemies for your frame
    - lots of Eximus with overguard in Steel Path = ideal situation for this arcane
    - very good synergy with status DoT weapons (damage over time) and/or AoE weapons (area of effect), ex: [Furis Incarnon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Furis_Incarnon_Genesis), [Akarius Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Akarius_Prime), [Kuva Nukor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Nukor), [Dual Toxocyst incarnon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dual_Toxocyst_Incarnon_Genesis)
- [Rakta Dark Dagger](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rakta_Dark_Dagger): generates shields when attacking irradiated enemies (weapon has base radiation). Good in pure electric influence, elec/blast, elec/gas builds. Passive doesn't work at 0 shields, sometimes need to generate some by choosing from the following solutions:
    - [Brief Respite](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brief_Respite)
    - [Augur mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Augur_Mods)
    - [Fast Deflection](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fast_Deflection) + [Vigilante Vigor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vigilante_Vigor)
    - bonus: being a dagger, works well with [Amalgam Argonak](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam_Argonak_Metal_Auger) to add armor strip to the weapon
- [Energizing Shot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energizing_Shot) + secondary weapon + shoot an energy orb + [Equilibrium](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equilibrium) / [purple shard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard) = instant self-revive
   - if needed add Protea specter / Kuva Zarr crewmate to generate orbs
   - if needed equip [Undying Will](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Undying_Will)
- [Arcane Reaper](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Reaper):
    - max rank: +660 armor / +24 hp/sec for 10s. Can stack multiple times
    - niche: requires a healthtank build + a weapon that already overkills without arcane (ex: Valkyr claws). Will prefer influence or arcane exposure most of the time
