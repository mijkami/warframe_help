# Operator

## **Focus**
- **Unairu** allows you to be immune to certain controls (Poise, first talent) and self-rez (Last Gasp)
- **Vazarin** can make you invincible (Protective Sling)
- **Zenurik** for energy regen if needed (find other sources as soon as possible)
- **Madurai** useful for operator damage later in the game (boost Tenno damage + power/speed cast for frame)

---------- 

## **Survival** 
The operator can contribute to warframe survival:

- **self-resurrection**:
    - via the talent [Last Gasp](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Unairu#Last_Gasp) of the Unairu Focus school
    - allows **bringing the warframe back to life** by killing 3 enemies as operator within 15 seconds
    - available for all operator branches: **unlock the passives linked to the path** concerned to access them permanently (max rank). Other [Way-Bounds passives](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus#Way-Bounds) allow increasing operator damage & survival making resurrection easier.
    - Madurai active skills facilitate operator kills for rez
    - the glaive (Phahd, secondary attack) from the amplifier facilitates rez
- **preventive protection**:
    - via the Vazarin talent [Protective Sling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Vazarin#Protective_Sling)
    - allows making allied targets (including your warframe) invulnerable for 5s after "dashing" through
    - use [Magus Cloud](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magus_Cloud) to facilitate the operation
    - very particular / repetitive gameplay & requires Tektolyst unlocked for lvl cap (enemies lvl 9999)
- **enemy control**:
    - via the operator arcane [Magus Lockdown](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magus_Lockdown)
    - an operator dash allows creating a mine (max 2) that blocks up to 10 enemies each for 4 seconds within 15 meters

## **Arcanes**
This will mainly be the operator that sometimes needs to be adapted to the content.

- **[operator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Enhancement#Operator_Arcanes)**:
    - cascade: [Magus Cloud](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magus_Cloud) + other (ex: [Magus Lockdown](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magus_Lockdown))
    - disruption: [Magus Lockdown](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magus_Lockdown) + other
    - others:
        - [Magus Melt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magus_Melt): +damage for Zariman Angels + operator rez
        - [Magus Anomaly](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Magus_Anomaly): attracts enemies, useful for certain bounties/missions (Deimos)
        - [Emergence Dissipate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Emergence_Dissipate): energy regen
- **[amp](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Enhancement#Amp_Arcanes)**:
    - 1st slot mandatory: [Eternal Eradicate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eternal_Eradicate)
    - 2nd slot to choose from:
        - [Eternal Onslaught](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eternal_Onslaught)
        - [Virtuous Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Virtuos_Strike)
        - [Virtuous Shadow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Virtuos_Shadow)
        - [Eternal Logistics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eternal_Logistics)

## **Tektolyst / Tauron Artifacts**
[Tektolyst artifacts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tektolyst_Artifact) are "ultimate weapons" for operators:

- give access to new [mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tektolyst_Artifact#Tektolyst_Artifact_Mods) and [arcanes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tektolyst_Artifact#Tektolyst_Artifact_Arcanes) applying to operator weapons:
    - **big damage boost**, makes "viable" all operator schools at lvl cap (enemies lvl 9999). Without Tektolyst, Madurai is "mandatory" for Cascade cap.
    - arcanes impacting warframe weapons (fire boost on secondary weapons)
    - buffs and ultimate attacks interesting but too random / little used as is
- accessible after completing the quest [The Old Peace](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Old_Peace), in drops from the gameplay mode [Rebellion Perita](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Perita_Rebellion) and [Marie's](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Marie_Leroux) shop

    - [Omni-Evi](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omn-Evi): crit chance / crit damage
    - [Hok-Kaal](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hok-Kaal): operator control protection
    - [Hayan-Dabor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hayan-Dabor): multishot
    - [Vikla-Safor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vikla-Safor): Fire Rate + Ammo Efficiency
- Arcane: [Zid-An Uskos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Zid-An_Uskos): Operator kills give a permanent buff up to +250% fire damage on secondary weapon (of warframe)
