

# ARMOR STRIP

[Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor) is often the most annoying enemy stat in Steel Path, it increases a lot there (SP boost + level scaling).

Having [armor strip](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor#Removing_Enemy_Armor_(Armor_Stripping)) (means to remove armor) is very useful. It is not mandatory to remove armor completely today and this since [the Jade patch](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_36#Update_36.0).

---------- 

## **Sources**


### **Common Sources**
- [corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage) status:
    - without archon shard: up to 80% strip on non-boss
    - with [archon shard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard): up to 100% with 2 green non-tau shards. 1 tau shard allows 13 corrosive stacks and strip to 98%, more than enough today.
- [heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage) status: removes up to 50% armor
- [helminth](warframes.md/#helminth) skills:
    - Pillage (Hildryn, + shield strip from enemies + gives shield to frame + cleanses status)
    - Terrify (Nekros, + low power demand + instant application + no Line of Sight)
    - Tharros Strike (Styanax, + shield strip + instant application, - blocked by walls)
    - Ophanim Eyes (Jade, + slow)
- aura [Corrosive Projection](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Corrosive_Projection)


### **Niche Sources**
- [amalgam Argonak](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Amalgam_Argonak_Metal_Auger) mod + Daggers
- mod [Shattering Impact](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shattering_Impact), used on:
    - melee projectile weapons (Vastilok) for boss armor strip (Eidolons)
    - anti-acolyte stick (ex: Praedos crit build + Shattering Impact)
- other frames have [their own non-helminth armor strip](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor#Removing_Enemy_Armor_(Armor_Stripping))
- companions:
    - mechanical (Diriga, [Hound](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hound_(Companion)) of Parvos)> [priming](weapons.md/#priming) AoE: [Contagious Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Contagious_Bond) + [Manifold Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Manifold_Bond) + [Arc Coil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arc_Coil) (Diriga) / [Synergized Prospectus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Synergized_Prospectus) + [Repo Audit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Repo_Audit) + Corro/Heat on companion weapon
    - animals: [Vicious Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vicious_Bond) + [Swipe](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Swipe) (Kavat)
