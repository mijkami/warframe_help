# Steel Path Warframes

Focus on a very **small pool of warframes** at the beginning, preferably with good built-in survival tools without too much investment.

Force yourself to read the full descriptions of the frame's kit (Arsenal > Hover over Frame > Abilities: read passive, abilities and all tips).

Consider adding a [Helminth](#helminth) for damage or survival.

## FRAME MODS: SURVIVE

  - **health**, **armor** & **Adaptation** mod: 
    - without arcane: for early Steel Path this can help pass a threshold. Damage reduction skills can also help. After that it peters out (lvl 1500+ in endless missions) but that's not a problem here.
    - with [Arcane Persistence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Persistence): health/armor tanking viable at lvl cap.
      Prerequisites:
          - minimum 700 armor for activation. Count 1400 armor to handle heat or corrosive procs (1892 for proc+corro at same time but very rare). Consider blue archon shards.
          - take into account magnetic status and [nullifiers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nullifier_Crewman) that can **disable the arcane (!)**: 
              - **status**: either use cleanses (Saryn's Molt, [Rolling Guard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rolling_Guard) mod), or [status immunity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect#Sources_of_Status_Immunity) (Inaros' Scarab Armor, Trinity's Link, Titania's Helminth Spellbind / Trinity's Well of Life, etc...)
              - **nullifiers**: have enough AoE or melee weapons with lots of range and/or impact status. Consider a fast-firing weapon or one that can cancel shields (Miter with [Neutralizing Justice](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neutralizing_Justice))
  - **Shieldgating**:
    - **Catalyzing Shields + Brief Respite / Augur mods**: active/dynamic gameplay and energy-demanding, so need good management (**Flow + Equilibrium** and/or little **Efficiency** and/or Helminth **Nourish** from Grendel, for example)
    - **Primed Redirection + Guardian** (companion mod): expensive technique, complements with Vigilante Vigor + Fast Deflection or even Arcane Aegis if you want to push it. For the companion with Guardian, prefer a Diriga (Arc Coil + Guardian + Manifold Bond + Momentous Bond + (Primed) Regen, NO Assault Mode) and a weapon full of status (Prime Laser Rifle + Viral/Fire/Mag/Rad)
    - **Omamori (Helminth) + Catalyzing Shields + Vigilante Vigor + Fast Deflection** +/- Arcane Aegis: quasi-invulnerability passive as long as Omamori is maintained
  - **[Invisibility](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Invisible)**: very strong but sensitive to stray bullets (bad positioning) and damage over time ([Rolling Guard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rolling_Guard) compensates)
    - Frame ability: Ash, Ivara, Loki, Octavia, Oraxia, Voruna, Wisp, Wukong, Cyte-09
    - [Helminth](#helminth):
        - [Evade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Evade) from Cyte, little compatible with group play
        - [Quiver](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quiver) from Ivara, reserved for static or haphazard gameplay (possible to plant the arrow in your sentinel)
    - [Companions](pets.md): for "caster" frames that don't need to use weapons (Protea, Xaku, Dante...)
        - [Shade Prime](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ghost): more solid (less aggro), access to Guardian mod
        - [Huras Kubrow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stalk): free companion that can die more easily, gives access to retriever mods + Kubrow nuke


## ENERGY

In most cases, running out of energy is synonymous with death: either you no longer kill dangerous enemies, or you no longer cast skills to feed shieldgating.

Two main strategies that can be combined:

- reduce the cost of skills:
   - **efficiency**: direct reduction
      - [Arcane Sculptor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Sculptor)
        - **frame** (very) compatible: Cyte, Follie, Gara, Gyre, Jade, Protea, Vauban, Volt
        - **helminth** (very) compatible: **Brightbonnet** (Nokko), **Molt** (Saryn), **Astral Shell** (Sirius & Orion)
   - **duration**: for buffs, fewer casts / minute => less energy spent
- increase the stock/**energy pool** and ways to refill it
   - [energy pool](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Capacity#Increasing_Energy_Capacity): Flow, Primed Flow, blue archon shards (2-3 max)
   - **[energy supply](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Capacity#Restoring_Energy)**:
      - **common / meta**:
        - drop + conversion health orbs: [companion](pets.md) AoE + [**Synth Deconstruct**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Synth_Deconstruct) + health > energy conversion ([**Equilibrium**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Equilibrium), [purple shards](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard))
        - [helminth skill](#helminth):
            - [**Nourish**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nourish) from Grendel, combo possible with [Energy Nexus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Nexus) and/or [Archon Stretch](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Stretch) (proc with [Arc Coil](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arc_Coil) from Diriga or elec on companion claws)
            - [Brightbonnet](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Brightbonnet) from Nokko. The rebound of the projectile from the skill or frame on the mushrooms created allows speeding up energy regeneration. Think about "spamming" against a wall to make it rebound on mushrooms very quickly. Excellent combo with [Arcane Sculptor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Sculptor).
        - [**Arcane Energize**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Energize) most expensive/difficult to farm today but very effective (prioritize reroll [Vosfor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vosfor), event drops or plats... [Eidolon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=25LlzizpO3I) runs as last resort)
        - kill an [Eximus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eximus#Overview): guaranteed energy orb drop
        - [Large Energy Restore](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Squad_Energy_Restore#Large) ("pizza"): bind to a shortcut in the consumable wheel + keyboard/gamepad shortcut. Can be spammed in normal, cooldown in SP. x100 craft blueprint available in Dojo.
      - uncommon:
        - [archon shard yellow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard): +50% efficiency on energy orbs (+75% for tau-forged)
        - Parazon: make a finisher with [Blood for Energy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blood_For_Energy)
        - [Non-Helminth Skills](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Capacity#Abilities)
        - [Duplex Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Duplex_Bond) + [companion](pets.md) that hits hard in melee
        - Focus school [Zenurik](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Zenurik) [Energy Pulse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Zenurik#Energy_Pulse) (incompatible with certain game modes requiring Madurai)
        - Arcane [Primary Exhilarate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primary_Exhilarate) on main weapon primer (ex: Bubonico with forced impact + AoE)
        - Arcane [Emergence Dissipate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Emergence_Dissipate) for operator
        - [Energy Generator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Energy_Generator) from Dethcube companion
        - [Xata Invocation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xata_Invocation) from the Grimoire (secondary weapon)
        - [Rage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rage) / [Hunter Adrenaline](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunter_Adrenaline): losing health often means getting one-shot at a certain point in Steel Path, avoid except in special cases
        - [Entropy](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entropy) effect from Cephalon Suda faction: passively by gaining XP while carrying weapons/mods with the effect, generates 25% max energy for 30s and gives 25% energy. Notably [Entropy Burst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Entropy_Burst) from Supra Vandal which can make a energy regen rifle and anti-nullifier shield weapon thanks to high firerate.

## HELMINTH
Unlock [**Helminth**](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth#Ability_Replacement) on Deimos to apply abilities to other frames and free up frame slots without uselessly "throwing away" them.

### Priority Helminth Abilities
Very strong, common & meta:

- **damage** (stat buffs): 
   - **[Roar](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Roar)** from Rhino to boost anti-faction, synergizes with damage over time (fire, blast, toxin, elec)
   - **[Xata](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xata%27s_Whisper)** from Xaku to add Void damage (many interactions + Void bonus damage on overguard + magnetic bubble effect)
   - **[Nourish](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nourish)** from Grendel to boost energy gain, Viral bonus, light CC
   - **[Wrathful Advance](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wrathful_Advance)** from Kullervo to buff melee crit %, reverse the cast via preferences and aim at the ground
- **tanking**: 
   - **[Pillage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pillage)** from Hildryn for shield gain + armor/shield strip + cleanse status
   - **[Omamori](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Omamori)** from Koumei for pseudo-dodge (very strong in addition to dodge/shield-gating)
- **armor strip**: less priority today, makes enemies more fragile:
   - **[Pillage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Pillage)** from Hildryn, shield and armor strip, requires lots of power (rather with Corrosive Projection aura) + blocked by walls, long cast
   - **[Terrify](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Terrify)** from Nekros, armor only, no LoS, requires little power, fast cast, costs quite a lot of energy
   - **[Tharros Strike](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tharros_Strike)** from Styanax, armor and shield strip, LoS, fast cast, heals health, short range
   - **[Ophanim Eyes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ophanim_Eyes)** from Jade, armor only, continuous debuff in vision cone, little energy consumed + enemy slow

### Niche Helminth Abilities
Sometimes useful, much less visible/prevalent:

- **damage** (stat buffs): 
    - [Eclipse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eclipse): increases base damage (like a Serration mod). Heavily nerfed in the past, can be useful. Multiplicative damage buff applied after the rest. Double bonus for melee projectiles (Exodia Contagion, [Glaives](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Glaive_(Weapon_Type)))
    - [Thermal Sunder](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Thermal_Sunder) from Gauss + augment mod [Thermal Transfer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Thermal_Transfer) to add Blast
    - [Shock](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shock) from Volt + [Shock Trooper](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shock_Trooper): electric damage boost
    - [Warcry](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Warcry) from Valkyr: melee attack speed buff (mostly) and armor (a bit)
    - [Breach Surge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Breach_Surge) from Wisp: multiplies damage received from one target to inflict on another, light CC (radiation + blind, does not affect Eximus)
    - [Airburst](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Airburst) from Zephyr + [Airbust Rounds](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Airburst_Rounds): secondary weapon damage buff, requires hitting many targets (range) often an anti-synergy
  - **damage** (debuff):
    - [Webbed Embrace](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Webbed_Embrace) from Oraxia: zone CC (3 zones max) + vulnerability debuff (= increased damage received)
    - [Sentient Wrath](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentient_Wrath) from Caliban: cone CC + vulnerability debuff
    - [Rest & Rage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rest_%26_Rage) from Equinox (Day version, requires a clear emission color on the frame): enemy speed boost + vulnerability debuff
- **tanking**:
  - **buff** / frame effect:
    - [Molt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Molt) from Saryn: light aggro (instant part), speed boost, status cleanse & instant cast (useful for shield-gating)
    - [Null Star](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Neutron_Star) from Nova: 75% DR + enemy CC
    - [Condemn](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condemn) from Harrow: shield regen + enemy CC + easy headshots
    - [Quiver](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Quiver) from Ivara, allows creating static invisibility zones. Very particular gameplay. Possibility to link the zone to a sentinel, hard to achieve consistently in dangerous situations
    - [Eclipse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Eclipse): 75% damage reduction. Can be shared with allies via [Total Eclipse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Total_Eclipse)
  - **enemy control**:
    - [Resonator](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resonator) from Octavia, completely disables enemy AI. Requires lots of range
    - [Astral Shell](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Astral_Shell) from Sirius & Orion: automatically generates when hit invulnerable decoys that draw fire to them within 30m. Couple with a bit of shield regen. Compatible with [Arcane Sculptor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Sculptor)
    - [Silence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Silence) from Banshee: disables enemy abilities (including Eximus auras) + short stun
    - [Shooting Gallery](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shooting_Gallery) from Mesa + [Muzzle Flash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Muzzle_Flash): very good CC but no longer affects Eximus, small damage buff too
    - [Gloom](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Gloom) from Sevagoth, important slow + life steal, big energy drain
    - [Webbed Embrace](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Webbed_Embrace) from Oraxia: zone CC (3 zones max) + vulnerability debuff (= increased damage received)
- **energy**: (other than Nourish...)
    - [Dispensary](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Dispensary) from Protea: generation of health/energy orbs + ammo at a fixed point
    - [Fractured Blast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fractured_Blast) from Citrine for health/energy orb generation
    - [Lycath's Hunt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Lycath%27s_Hunt) from Voruna for health orb generation on melee attacks / energy on headshots (use Equilibrium + melee)


## STATUS RESISTANCE
[Status](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect) from enemies has 3 main dangers:

  - damage over time: especially toxin, fire, slash
  - controls: the most annoying are [Knockdown](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Knockdown) and [Stagger](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stagger) blocking you in place
  - energy drain via [Magnetic](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Magnetic_Damage) status, less common but potentially deadly

You can protect yourself in different ways via [immunities](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect#Sources_of_Status_Immunity) or [cleanses/removals](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect#Sources_of_Status_Effect_Cleansing). Requires specific frame kits or external sources ([Helminth](#helminth), mods, companions, arcanes).

**Control Immunity**:

![
"Huge DPS Increase"](../assets/steelpath/PSF.gif){ width="150" align=right }

- Not mandatory, strongly **recommended**. In Steel Path there are + more Eximus and a greater density of mobs: + dangerous controls. Being immune prevents dying while a mob knocks you down without being able to react
- Have a frame immunized via skills or [Overguard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Overguard) (see [recommended frames](#recommended-frames))
- Use [Primed Sure Footed](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primed_Sure_Footed) or [Handspring](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Handspring) for controls
- avoid [Sure Footed (non-prime)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sure_Footed)
  - if PSF impossible, prefer [Handspring](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Handspring), only takes one mod
  - [Sure Footed (non-prime)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sure_Footed) + [Fortitude](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fortitude) possible for 100% immunity but takes one slot and "breaks" your builds, rarely feasible (unless survival already based on passive shield regen)
- [Negate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Negate) from [Wyrm](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Wyrm) in combo with [Manifold Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Manifold_Bond) to reduce cooldown. Requires being very comfortable with DPS and being able to keep your companion alive / bring it back quickly ([Momentous Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Momentous_Bond) + [Primed Regen](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primed_Regen) and [others](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Death#Companions))

## MOBILITY

[Maneuvers](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers) are the basis of Warframe movement: bullet-jumps, rolls, slides... [reference video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WoKVEisXJhE)

Fundamental part of the game for:

- the fun of the game
- movement speed
- survivability
   - the roll provides 75% damage reduction, or even invulnerability via [Rolling Guard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rolling_Guard)
   - fast movement greatly reduces the probability of being hit / enemy accuracy
   - [Survival Instinct](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Survival_Instinct) from Sly Vulpaphyla even resets enemy targeting AI every 5 seconds via a bullet jump

Common sources:

- **[Velocity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers#Parkour_Velocity)**: major impact, increases bullet-jump and roll distances
    - [Praedos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Praedos): 30% (+speed boost)
    - [Archon shard yellow](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Shard): +15 / +22.5%
    - [velocity mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Maneuvers#Parkour_Velocity): relatively niche, can replace PSF in exilus on some builds if you have invulnerability or are comfortable
    - exilus [Proton Pulse](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Proton_Pulse) for experienced Cascade players
