## What is Steel Path?

You need to understand what [Steel Path](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Steel_Path) is to adapt correctly:

- "hard mode" unlocked after completing [starchart](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Star_Chart) missions accessible before the [New War](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_New_War) quest
- activates in navigation to the right of the screen in the middle, once unlocked
- enemies are [reinforced](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Level_Scaling):
    - +100 level, 250% health and shields (archwing +50 level)
    - MAX enemy spawn density in endless missions (survival, cascade etc) as for a 4-player max squad in normal
    - [Acolyte](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Acolytes) spawns every 3-7 minutes
- loot is improved:
    - +100% resource drop chance, +100% mod drop chance
    - acolytes drop [Steel Path arcanes](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Enhancement) + Steel Essence
    - opening a relic in SP gives 1 bonus Steel Essence
    - increased drop rate of Riven slivers on Eximus ([Iron Wake](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Riven_Sliver#Usage) shop)
    - more generous token drops in their respective missions (Pathos clamps Duviri, Kullervo, Dante, Citrine, Voruna, Dagath, Jade, Koumei, Temple)

### Steel Essence
[Steel Essence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Steel_Essence) earned gives you access to [Teshin's shop](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Steel_Path#Steel_Essence):

- relic packs
- Kuva packs (reroll rivens)
- [weekly rotation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Steel_Path#Teshin's_Steel_Path_Honors_shop_-_Weekly_Rotating_Offer)
    - Umbra Forma blueprint
    - large Endo pack
    - large Kuva pack
    - rivens

Main sources:

- [Acolyte](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Acolytes) drops in Steel Path missions give 2 Steel Essence
    - think about stacking boosters: 1 drop booster will give 4 essence per acolyte, having a retriever mod will double to 8 per acolyte
    - doing long fissure relics increases even further the essence received per acolyte: you will get more essence doing 1 hour of survival at once than an hour of chaining Steel Path captures
- do Steel Path incursions in your alerts (requires having selected Steel Path in navigation) for 5 essence: some are quick and there's often people participating
- completing a Steel Path planet gives 25 essence
- opening a relic in Steel Path gives 1 essence
- etc...
