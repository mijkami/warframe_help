# Steel Path Weapons

Focus on **a small pool of well-modded weapons**.

Do the [Steel Path circuit](../weekly-checklist.en.md#confirmed-players) to get [Incarnons](#incarnons) (with decrees and in group it happens early), or [Liches](#lich-weapon-choices) for some weapons. Outside of the circuit, Incarnon weapons from [Zariman](../farm/syndicates.en.md#mini-hub-syndicates) are also very good.

## **Incarnons**
All [Incarnons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Incarnon) have potential, but some are more effective in the current state of the game and/or more enjoyable to play in general.

!!! note "checklist MANDATORY prerequisites to get an Incarnon weapon"
    - Zariman Incarnons: quest [Angels of the Zariman](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Angels_of_the_Zariman)
    - Sanctum Incarnons: quest [Whispers in the Walls](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Whispers_in_the_Walls) + [Deadlock Protocol](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Deadlock_Protocol)
    - Duviri Incarnons: quest [Duviri Paradox](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Duviri_Paradox) + [SteelPath](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Steel_Path)

### **Priority Incarnons**
In doubt, choose the following Incarnons:

- **native** (without adapter):
      - **primaries** / **secondaries** from Zariman: Felarx, Phenmor, Laetum
      - **melee**:
         - Zariman: Praedos (especially for speed, DPS OK), Innodem (decent)
         - Duviri: Thalys (good range / Influence combo)
- **adapters** in the [Steel Path Duviri Circuit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Circuit#The_Steel_Path_Circuit) (SP circuit tip: get a weapon (melee priority) with Condition Overload or Galvanized Shot/Aptitude and take elemental damage decrees):
      - primaries / secondaries: Torid, Dual toxocyst, Furis, Burston, Boar, Lex Prime
      - melee: Dual Ichor, Okina Prime, Nami Solo, Hate, Bo Prime, Magistar

## **Lich Weapons**
Many possible weapons, some more fun than others.
3 different types of Liches currently:

- Grineer: [Kuva Lich](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Lich)
- Corpus: [Sister of Parvos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos)
- Infested: [Technocyte Coda](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Technocyte_Coda)

!!! note "checklist MANDATORY prerequisites to get a Lich"

    - be [MR 5](../beginner/mastery-rank.md) (since [Update 35](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_35#Update_35.0) of 2023)
    - have done [War Within](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_War_Within) + [Rising Tide](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rising_Tide)
    - not have an active lich (only one at a time)
        - to check: go in game menu and see bottom right if you have a red/blue/green button next to your Nightwave


!!! note "Displayed MR requirement vs real"

      There are 2 types of Lich weapons:

    - **Lich weapons obtained from Foundry** after battle (Kuva + Tenet)
        - ignore MR requirements, only need MR 5 + [War Within](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_War_Within) + [Rising Tide](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Rising_Tide)
    - **Lich weapons obtained from in-game shops** with tokens (Coda weapons + Tenet from Ergo Glast)
        - follow normal MR requirement rules to obtain the weapon
            - at MR 16 you have unlocked all [Tenet from Ergo Glast](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ergo_Glast#Tenet_Weapons) (MR 14 to 16 depending on weapons)
            - at MR 17 you have unlocked all [Coda](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Coda_Weapons) from Eleanor (MR 17 for all)

### **Status / Progenitors**
Lich weapons benefit from a damage/status bonus from +20 to +60%, whose status is determined by the frame that generated the Lich (larva, candidate...). We call this frame the progenitor.

#### **Status Types**
See [Progenitor list](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Lich/Progenitor) for details.
Example: use Saryn for a toxin weapon, Nezha for a fire weapon, Mesa for a magnetic weapon.

These status are less critical to create a weapon than in the past: they can be changed with an [Elemental Vice](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Elemental_Vice).

!!! note "Optimal Status: Kuva / Tenet"
      See the list of [recommended status](https://i.hep.gg/NemesisProgenitors).

??? note "Optimal Status: Coda"

    Can change according to meta/knowledge state, just for reference:

    - **primaries**:
        - _Bassocyst_: **Cold/Fire** (make Blast)
        - _Hema_: **Magnetic**
        - _Sporothrix_: **Elec** (goal: Blast + Elec)
        - _Synapse_: **Fire** (goal: Corro / Blast or Corro/Fire)
    - **secondaries**:
        - _Tysis_: **Magnetic**
        - _Pox_: **Cold** (goal: Corro / Blast with Primed Heated Charge)
        - _Dual Coda Torxica_: **Elec** OR **Fire** (goal: Viral Fire, Elec Viral)
        - _Catabolyst_: **Fire** (goal: Corro+Fire+Viral)
    - **melee**:
        - _Pathocyst_: **Elec** (Blast + Elec Influence)
        - _Mire_: **Elec** (Gas+Elec Influence) or Fire (Gas Afflictions)
        - _Hirudo_: **Elec** (Viral+Elec Influence)
        - _Caustacyst_: **Elec** (Viral+Elec Influence)
        - _Motovore_: **Impact** (Corro Exposure) or **Elec** (Influence)


#### **Status Percentages**

For shop weapons (Coda, Tenet from Ergo Glast), to reduce the number of fusions before max, target weapons [with the following %](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Weapons#Elemental_Bonus):

   - directly 60% (nice)
   - 52.8% (just 1 fusion before max)
   - 48% (2 fusions before max)

### **Getting Lich Weapons**
??? note "Grineer Liches (Kuva)"

      - be MR5 minimum, have done [The War Within](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_War_Within)
      - spawn a [Kuva Larvling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Larvling) in Grineer missions lvl 20+, preferably solo on Cassini (Saturn)
      - missions to do are:
         - level 55 to 110 depending on your lich level
         - locations: Earth, Mars, Ceres, Sedna, Kuva Fortress (missions + [Requiem relics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Relic#Requiem_Relics))
         - do Lich-infested missions in public preferably (allies help advance Requiem word discovery)
      - weapon level requirement is ignored
      - requires opening [Requiem relics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Relic#Requiem_Relics) obtained by killing thralls from the lich in mission (or doing Kuva Siphon alerts) to get [Requiem mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Requiem_Mods)

      - once the right combination is found, beat the lich in Railjack mission on [Saturn Proxima](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Saturn_Proxima). Use Revenant and/or Silence and a fast-firing weapon (Dual Toxocyst Incarnon) to make it easier. Kill the lich to get the weapon.

## **Priming**
[Priming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oj1SnZOxBp4) consists of "preparing" enemies with a status source so that another damage weapon benefiting from this status can destroy it even more powerfully just after. Big damage boost potential, can help pass a threshold (Steel Path trash mobs, mini-bosses like Demolysts in Disruption).

Also see interaction with [CO / GunCO mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload_(Mechanic)) (**condition overload**/status overload, Galvanized Shot, Galvanized Aptitude).

#### **Primers: Examples**

  - secondary weapons: Epitaph, Kompressa (Prime), (Coda) Tysis...
  - primaries: Cedo Prime, Bubonico...
  - companion:
      - Diriga (see [Companions](pets.md))
      - [Sentinel](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sentinel) / [Moa](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/MOA_(Companion)) + [Tazicor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Tazicor) (mini Nukor) + [Duplex Bond](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Duplex_Bond)

### **Primers: Stats to look for**

- multishot
- fire rate
- status chance
- [status effect](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect):
      - **base**:
         - Viral / Magnetic (single 1999 mod): increases damage to health / shields & overguard
         - Corro / Fire: reduces enemy armor
      - **bonus**: Radiation (single Entrati/Deimos lab mod): random control + adds a status via single mod for [CO/GunCo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload_(Mechanic))
      - **others**:
         - Cold: slows Demolysts + increases crit damage received (+0.5 crit multiplier at 9 stacks)
         - Puncture: reduces enemy damage (80% reduction at 5 stacks) + increases final crit chance received (+25% at 5 stacks)
      - **arcanes**: [Secondary Encumber](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Secondary_Encumber)
