

## General

- there are currently 35 account mastery ranks in the game.
- most weapons/frames require a minimum Mastery Rank (MR). At level 16 you have access to 99% of content.
- maxing to 30/30 an item gives you the maximum mastery for your account.
- think about maxing "trash" items whose blueprints only cost a few credits and that you will want to throw away later to free up a slot
- build as many weapons and warframes as possible to level up mastery, you can throw away the easiest to obtain
- do NOT throw away your non-prime frames directly, farm [helminth](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth_Segment) on [Deimos](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deimos) first, you will need to feed it your non-prime frames
- you can build an item in advance and pick it up from the foundry much later
- consult [Farm](../farm/index.md) for the resources and XP needed for your new frames & weapons

Mainly unlocks:

- Quests (rank 3, 4, 5, 6)
- Trading (rank 2)
- [Syndicates](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicate) (rank 3)
- [Sorties](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sortie) (rank 5)
- access [Kuva Liches](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Kuva_Lich) and [Corpus Liches](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sisters_of_Parvos) as well as their weapons (rank 5)
- purchase [Helminth Segment](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Helminth_Segment) (rank 8)

Each rank [also unlocks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mastery_Rank#Other_Benefits):

   - +1 [trade](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Trading) max
   - +50 [void traces](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Traces) max threshold
   - +500 [daily syndicate reputation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Syndicate) max
   - +5000 [daily focus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus) max

## Blueprints / Blueprints

The crux of the matter. Many sources of acquisition, always have items in construction in your foundry to regularly increase your Mastery.

/!\ Some blueprints [require other weapons](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Weapons_Required_as_Crafting_Ingredients). Do NOT throw away a non-prime weapon until you've checked the list above!

- _market_: the market in your orbiter gives access to lots of weapons, even if the price is shown in plats, click on it and buy the plan/blueprint in credits. Some basic weapons can be bought already built for credits
- _dojo_: joining a clan gives access to different labs and a lot of weapons and some warframes. Think about doing [Invasions]() to obtain rare resources for some weapons.
- _boss_: all bosses drop frame plans or even weapon plans, think about doing them multiple times to get all necessary plans
- _reputation_: [syndicates]() have reputations that will give you access to weapons and parts
- [_open-worlds_](starchart.md/#open-worlds): special syndicates asking to do [bounties / bounty missions]() in open worlds, bounties drops and reputations offer lots of weapon and frame plans
