## What is Warframe?

[How to Warframe - 5 min video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yghvq5878QI)

Warframe is a looter-shooter with space ninjas, oriented towards power fantasy (you become super-mega-strong at exploding hordes of enemies).
You kill lots of things in many ways while collecting resources to become more and more efficient and varied.

On the menu:

- **shooting** with a very varied and customizable arsenal in substance (efficiency) and form (skins, fashion-frame)
- **loot** / farm (weapons, armors (warframes), resources)
- **ninja** style (excellent mobility, bullet-jumps, wall jumps etc...)
- **space**: the entire star system and strange corners to explore

It is also possible to farm even more to exchange premium currency (plats/platinum) with other players, the economy is very developed and you can buy lots of premium stuff while being "Free to play".
