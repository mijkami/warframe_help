# Resources

Identify good spots, use the right tools and playing in a group will be more profitable.

To identify resource sources in-game you can:

- hover over a resource in a Foundry blueprint, the text will give you info
- click in navigation on a planet and at the bottom right access the resource extractor interface, you will see the basic droppable resources on the planet
- search for your resource on the [Warframe wiki](https://wiki.warframe.com), see [References](../content.md) for more info




---------------

## **Boosters**
Very important, stacking **boosters** will allow you to get much more resources in the same amount of time.
The [resource booster](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resource_Booster) (orange) is the most important, others are not worth the plat cost unless you already have a good [plat](plats.md) income, which will come later.

Resource booster sources (increases resources from pickups):

- [farm frames](../beginner/warframes.en.md#farm-frames)
- [companions](../beginner/pets.md)
- in-game shop: [resource booster](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resource_Booster). Costs plats, after that slot purchase is a priority buy that will allow you to accelerate your resource farm AND your plat farm (virtuous circle). 30-day boosters are the most cost-effective in cost/day.
- [endless relics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Fissure#Endless_Missions) (survival, defense...) : accumulates up to +100% resource pickups
- [True Master's Font](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/True_Master%27s_Font), blessing from MR 30+ player in relays (go to relay to AFK / take a break and grab some blessings)

Resource drop chance boosters sources (increases resources drops on the ground):

- [steel path](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Steel_Path) : booster +100% drop chances
- in-game shop : [booster +100% chances de drop](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Resource_Drop_Chance_Booster). Applies to whole group once. (if 4 players have it, it only applies +100% on enemies drops not 400%).
- [endless relics](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Void_Fissure#Endless_Missions) (survival, defense...) : accumulates up to +100% resource drop chances

If you have no boosters do not despair. The most important is to know they exist to give you a medium-term goal to acquire them and benefit earlier.

### **Open-World**
Fishing, mining & conservation: sources of fish, ores & tokens.
Do not forget your animal for [double-drop](resources.md/#boosters). A tanky anti-CC frame (Rhino) or even invisible (Ivara, long duration) will make the experience more enjoyable.

#### Fishing
!!! note "Fishing: details"
    - Gear:
        - get [fishing gear](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fishing#Gear) (spears, baits) from [Hai-Luk](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hai-Luk) (Cetus), [The Business](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Business) (Fortuna) and [Daughter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Daughter) (Necralisk)
        - equip your fishing spear in Arsenal, consumable wheel
        - get [Luminous Dye](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Luminous_Dye) on Cetus to see fish well
    - locations & fishing periods per fish: refer to [wiki page on fishing](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fishing)
        - [Cetus Spots](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/Fishingmap.png?059c6)
        - [Fortuna Spots](https://wiki.warframe.com/images/FortunaFishingMap.jpg?636d9)
    - in open-world of your choice:
        - think about using baits if needed
        - look for [hotspots](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fishing#/media/File:Warframe-Fish-Hotspot.gif) and use your [baits](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fishing#Gear) if needed (purchasable from NPCs)
        - Ivara's [Ensnare](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ensnare) above water can make the experience easier
        - Volt's passive allows one-shotting all fish without worrying about adapting your spear

#### Mining
!!! note "Mining: details"
    - only 2 ore colors per open-world
    - buy the mining tool directly from Fortuna (can be upgraded, superior to Cetus one)
    - using the secondary fire with the mining tool allows zooming like with a sniper
    - aim at the center of "rectangles" for better drops. The smaller/harder the rectangle, the better the reward.

#### Alternatives
!!! note "Alternative fast / mid-game farm"
    - **Fortuna**:
        - [Itzal Archwing Farm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWcexHvD3rM): crates near buildings and in "bunkers"
        - go into [Deck 12](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Deck_12) cave for mining: very few enemies and passing the "gate" in the cave allows resetting part of the cave infinitely
    - **Deimos**: 
        - [Itzal Archwing Farm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWcexHvD3rM): crates in the largest caves
        - Activate [Obelisks](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cambion_Drift#Requiem_Obelisks), wait for obelisk to debuff enemies and eliminate them with operator/necramech/Excalibur Umbra passive
