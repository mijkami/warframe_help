# Credits

Will mainly allow you to buy your blueprints in the Market and level up your mods.
Will also serve later for [Primed mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primed_Mods) bought from [Baro Ki'teer](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Baro_Ki%27Teer) or traded with other players (careful, the recipient still has to spend a 1 million trade tax in credits).

## **Boosters**

All credit boosters stack, do not deprive yourself and use as many as possible!

- you can get a [Retriever](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Category:Retriever_Mods) mod for your animal companions that gives a chance to double credits picked up from enemies
- the first mission of each day has a x2 boost on credits picked up
- the warframe [Chroma](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Chroma) will allow you to double credits picked up with its 4th ability, very useful in certain missions (Profit-Taker, 1999 caches)
- the [Credit Booster](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Credit_Booster), not necessary but appreciated


## **Sources**

### **Beginner**
- !!! note "selling duplicates"
    - escape > equipment > inventory. Dropdown menu on the right, select "duplicates". Sell duplicate frame parts you have that are not tradable (ex: "Harrow chassis", you will get plenty continuously)
    - /!\ mod sales: NEVER sell your mods for credits, the gain is limited and you should always prioritize Endo when dissolving duplicate mods
- farm Dark Sector: maps with additional boosts, one per planet (ex: Gabii on Ceres)
- farm [long missions](../beginner/starchart.md)
- Index on Neptune, go in group / public at the beginning to be accompanied
- Railjack: possibility to be accompanied in Railjack missions as soon as you have an Archwing, good source of credits/resources. Earth and Venus Proxima are quite accessible.

### **Confirmed**
- Credit Drops (use Chroma's ability 4):
    - Caches in Capture on 1999
    - Profit-Taker
- Railjack in the Veil (high-level)
